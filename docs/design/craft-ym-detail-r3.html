The convergence

This is r2's air + Linear's discipline: flat page, zero cards, one framed surface. Cost groups are separated by hairlines only; the summary flows straight out of the paper. Nothing floats except the one place a decision is made. The quiet rhythm you liked in Linear comes from spacing and restraint, not from surfaces.

One accent, held

Clay is the only voice: workflow stamp, add actions, the price underline, the block frame, the primary button. Iris appears once, on the glint. Status lives entirely in the one green chip. Everything else is ink on cream — Linear's monochrome-plus-one, in our palette.

Summary, fixed

The half-done card is gone. The summary is now typeset directly on the page, ruled off with a hairline — direct cost as the dominant figure, business share + full cost dashed beneath. It reads as the natural sum of the ledger above it, not a competing block.

Kept from every round

Lora returns on the name, price, and every figure — it's the signature that keeps this off the generic-SaaS shelf, and Linear's calm never depended on sans. Two-line airy rows and the single status chip ("Would be Healthy · 52%") both survive from r2. The arithmetic stays on the quiet second line.

If this is the one

This becomes the system: flat pages, hairline sections, one framed surface per screen where a decision happens, chips for status, Lora for outputs. Overview and Dashboard inherit it directly — and both are list-shaped, so Linear's calm will carry even further there than here.
