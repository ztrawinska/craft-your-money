Rows end in a verb (YNAB lesson)

Each attention row ends in a clay verb-link — "Reprice ›", "Set price ›" — using our Lucide chevron, so the row answers "what do I do?", not just "what's wrong?". No new button level: same semantics as our "+ Add material" and Reset links. The status chip shrinks into the meta line.

Empty = gone, not reassured

On a good day the section doesn't render at all — no green "all clear" band. The briefing already says "nice work"; a shorter, calmer screen says the rest. Confidence through absence.

Two states, not three

Attention is now binary: rows, or nothing. No third "empty-with-message" state to design, build, or maintain. Cleaner in the UI and in the code.

Still our language, not YNAB's

We took the structure (action per row, hide when empty) but kept our skin: flat, hairline rows, left-edge urgency stripe, one clay accent — no floating cards, no gradient. Structure borrowed, style ours.
